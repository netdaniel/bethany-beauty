About the studio

Meet Bethany

Bethany is a certified Bio Sculpture nail technician with a calm, detail-led approach to every set. She works with Bio Sculpture only — a flexible gel system that strengthens and protects the natural nail, rather than covering up the damage left behind by acrylics and hard gels.

Appointments are one-to-one and unhurried, so there is time to shape, refine and finish properly. From careful prep through to aftercare advice, the goal stays the same: healthy, clean beauty nail care that looks polished and feels kind to your own nails.

The Difference

Why it is Bio Sculpture only.

Bio Sculpture is a soft gel system built around the health of the natural nail. It is flexible, breathable and applied with care — which is exactly why it is the only system in this studio.

Flexible by design
The gel flexes with your nail instead of fighting it, so lifting and breakage are far less likely.
Nail-friendly prep
Gentle preparation and no aggressive drilling — your natural nail stays protected underneath.
True, lasting colour
A huge shade range with a finish that keeps its gloss and does not fade.
Considered formulas
Bio Sculpture does not test on animals — a product line that aligns with how I like to work.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you work with acrylic or other gel brands?

No. The studio is strictly Bio Sculpture — one professional gel system, used as it is designed to be used. No acrylics, and no mixing brands on the same nail.

How often will I need a refill?

Every 2–3 weeks keeps the set looking fresh and protects the natural nail. We will settle on a rhythm that suits how fast your nails grow.

Where is the studio?

It is a private home studio, one client at a time. The full address is shared as soon as your booking is confirmed.

How do I book, and how quickly will I hear back?

Send the form below, message on WhatsApp, or phone 074 740 9490. Requests are usually answered within a day.

How is pricing worked out?

It depends on the length and condition of your natural nails. Hand-painted art, a classic French or a chrome finish starts from R80, and your price is confirmed before any work begins.
